    What the agent did

    After the 6 February 2023 earthquakes, calls for help came in on every channel at once. The same building was reported dozens of times, addresses were misheard, and volunteers sorted it all by hand.

    Press play to watch the agent read each report, check the address against the official neighbourhood list and merge it into the right building. When something does not add up, it asks the person who reported it, reads the answer and updates the task. Select any report or task to see why.
